Companion resources for Chapter 2. Maintained here (not in the printed book) so they can be kept current.

**CHASE** = **C**larify & Scope · **H**igh-Level Design · **A**I/ML Deep Dive · **S**ystem & Infra Deep Dive · **E**xtensions & Trade-offs

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| [`phase-timebox-cheatsheet.md`](./phase-timebox-cheatsheet.md) | A printable one-page cheat sheet: the five CHASE phases, time boxes, what to do, and the output of each phase. |
| [`self-scoring-scorecard.md`](./self-scoring-scorecard.md) | A fillable scorecard (reusing the Chapter 1 rubric structure) to grade your own mock interviews and track progress. |
| [`signposting-and-recovery-scripts.md`](./signposting-and-recovery-scripts.md) | Ready-to-use signposting phrases, assumption statements, and scripts for recovering when you get stuck. |
| [`common-pitfalls-checklist.md`](./common-pitfalls-checklist.md) | A pre-interview checklist of the most common mistakes — with the fix for each. |
| [`common-doubts.md`](./common-doubts.md) | Answers to the questions candidates most often have about the framework, time management, and adapting to company styles. |
| [`key-terms.md`](./key-terms.md) | Chapter 2 glossary slice (CHASE, functional vs. non-functional requirements, HPA/KEDA, cache stampede, T-shaped answer, …). Feeds the cumulative book glossary. |
