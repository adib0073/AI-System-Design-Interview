# Chapter 1 — The AI System Design Interview Landscape

Companion resources for Chapter 1. These are maintained here (not in the printed book) so they can be kept current.

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| [`common-doubts.md`](./common-doubts.md) | Answers to the questions interviewees most often have about this round — which loop is it, ML vs. infra depth, "should I reach for an agentic/LLM design?", time management, and more. |
| [`interview-prep-resources.md`](./interview-prep-resources.md) | Curated, role-specific links: official engineering blogs, AI-research blogs, official YouTube channels, and company careers/interview-prep pages (Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, Apple, Netflix, and more). |
| [`role-depth-self-assessment.md`](./role-depth-self-assessment.md) | A "map your role → expected depth" checklist. Rate yourself per dimension and get a prioritized prep plan. |
| [`key-terms.md`](./key-terms.md) | Chapter 1 glossary slice (deterministic vs. non-deterministic, drift, serving tiers, feedback loops, seniority levels, …). Feeds the cumulative book glossary. |

> **Note on links:** URLs change. Anything in these files that points outside the repo is verified periodically but may drift — if you find a dead link, please open an issue or PR.
