# Chapter 16 — Notifications & Delivery Optimization

Companion resources for Chapter 16. The defining objective is **long-term engagement *minus* fatigue** — a notification that gets one click but causes an uninstall is a net loss. This chapter also weaves in GenAI/agentic angles (content generation, interruption policies).

Chapter 16 covers relevance ranking (value minus annoyance), volume optimization (fatigue budgets, frequency caps), send-time optimization, channel selection, consent/quiet hours, long-term holdouts, global send-budget arbitration, transactional vs. engagement notifications, and GenAI content generation + agentic interruption policies.
