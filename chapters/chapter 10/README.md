# Chapter 10 — Ads Ranking & Computational Advertising

Companion resources for Chapter 10. Ads ranking **reuses the Chapter 9 funnel** but adds the parts that make advertising unique: an **auction**, **money**, and **calibration**.

Chapter 10 covers auction mechanisms (GSP, second-price/Vickrey, VCG, RTB), eCPM ranking, pCTR/pCVR prediction (with delayed conversions and ESMM), the critical role of **calibration**, bid optimization/auto-bidding, budget pacing, ad quality/relevance, frequency capping, and privacy-preserving ads (ATT, Privacy Sandbox).
